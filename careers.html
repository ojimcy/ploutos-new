Senior Go (Golang) Developer

Remote
Senior Level
Full-Time
Hiring Now

We're seeking a Senior Go (Golang) Developer to join our core engineering team and help build mission-critical backend systems powering real-time payments, high-throughput APIs, and scalable microservices.

What You'll Do

  • Design, build, and maintain scalable backend services and APIs using Go
  • Lead development of high-performance microservices for payment processing, transaction routing, settlements, and wallets
  • Own service architecture, data models, caching layers, and fault-tolerant systems
  • Write well-tested, documented, and maintainable code following best practices
  • Mentor mid-level engineers and contribute to technical strategy
  • Collaborate with product, DevOps, and QA to drive continuous delivery
  • Optimize for reliability, low latency, and high throughput in production

Requirements

  • 5+ years experience building backend services, with 3+ years in Go
  • Deep expertise in Go idioms, concurrency patterns, and performance optimization
  • Experience building RESTful APIs, gRPC, or other service interfaces
  • Strong understanding of microservices, Docker, and Kubernetes
  • Proficient with SQL and NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, Redis, MongoDB)
  • Familiarity with message queues/event streaming (Kafka, NATS)
  • Strong code-review, documentation, and test-driven development skills

Nice to Have

  • Experience in payments/fintech, financial protocols, or compliance systems
  • Prior work with real-time processing, PCI-compliant systems, or banking integrations
  • Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) and serverless patterns
  • Knowledge of security best practices in fintech (OWASP, encryption, tokenization)
  • Experience with blockchain technology and smart contracts

Tech Stack

Go/Golang gRPC REST APIs PostgreSQL Redis MongoDB Docker Kubernetes Kafka AWS/GCP
