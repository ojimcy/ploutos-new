General Questions

PloutosLabs is a comprehensive fintech ecosystem designed to revolutionize digital finance in Nigeria and across Africa. Built on the BASE Network (Coinbase's Ethereum Layer 2 solution), we combine blockchain innovation with practical financial services including decentralized wallets, payment cards, and virtual card services.

PLTL is the native utility token of the PloutosLabs ecosystem, built on the BASE Network. It's used for transaction fees, staking rewards, governance, and accessing premium features across our products. The token has been audited by Cyberscope.io for security.

Yes, security is our top priority. Our smart contracts have been professionally audited by Cyberscope.io, and our team is KYC-verified. We implement industry-standard security practices including encrypted storage, secure authentication, and regular security assessments.

Skydda Wallet

Skydda Wallet is a complete decentralized wallet and trading platform within the PloutosLabs ecosystem. It offers self-custody (you control your private keys), multi-chain support, DEX integration for token swaps, and access to DeFi protocols.

Skydda Wallet supports multiple blockchain networks including Ethereum, BASE Network, Binance Smart Chain (BSC), and other EVM-compatible chains. We're continuously adding support for more networks.

When you create a Skydda Wallet, you'll receive a 12 or 24-word recovery phrase (seed phrase). Store this phrase securely offline. If you lose your device, simply install the app on a new device and use your recovery phrase to restore your wallet.

Payment Cards

KaviPay is a full payment card platform offering both virtual and physical cards in Nigeria, with features like ATM withdrawals and POS payments. Virtual Card is specifically designed for online transactions, subscriptions, and digital payments with instant activation and crypto funding.

You can fund your virtual card using cryptocurrency (with real-time conversion) or through bank transfer, depending on your location and the specific card product. The funds are converted to USD or your preferred currency instantly.

Our virtual cards are accepted at millions of online merchants worldwide that accept Visa or Mastercard. This includes Amazon, Netflix, Spotify, Google Play, Apple App Store, and many more. They're perfect for subscriptions, online shopping, and digital services.

Getting Started

Getting started is easy! Choose the product that fits your needs: visit skyddawallet.io for the decentralized wallet, kavipay.io for payment cards, or app.ploutoslabs.io for virtual cards. Create an account, complete verification if required, and start using our services.

PLTL tokens can be purchased on decentralized exchanges (DEXs) that support the BASE Network. Connect your wallet (like Skydda Wallet or MetaMask), ensure you have ETH on BASE for gas fees, and swap for PLTL. Always verify the official contract address from our whitepaper.

You can reach our support team through multiple channels: join our Telegram community at t.me/ploutoslabs for quick responses, follow us on Twitter @ploutoslabs for updates, or reach out through the support features within each product's app.
