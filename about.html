About PloutosLabs

Who We Are

PloutosLabs is a comprehensive fintech ecosystem designed to revolutionize digital finance in Nigeria and across Africa, with global expansion planned. Built on the BASE Network—Coinbase's Ethereum Layer 2 solution—our platform combines blockchain innovation with practical financial services to address the continent's most pressing financial inclusion challenges.

Our ecosystem encompasses Skydda Wallet, a fully decentralized trading platform, KaviPay for payment card services, and Virtual Cards for seamless online transactions. We're building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Our Mission

To support people and businesses through reliable and accessible financial and crypto services that create everyday utility and lasting impact. We aim to bridge the gap between traditional finance and blockchain technology, making digital financial services accessible to everyone.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading fintech ecosystem that empowers individuals and businesses with secure, innovative, and user-friendly financial solutions. We envision a future where everyone has access to modern financial tools, regardless of their location or economic status.
